Typographic Anomalies

Skeptics, sit down. Below are six pieces of evidence that I have collected with my own two eyes (and a borrowed camera). Each one is 100% real and definitely proof of alien life. No human could kern this well. Click any picture to see it BIG.

WARNING: Do not stare at Exhibit A for more than 30 seconds.


Exhibit A: The 27th Letter
Appeared on my practice sheet overnight, between M and N. It blinked. I am almost sure it blinked.
Exhibit B: The Wiltshire Q
A crop circle with a TAIL. Bracketed, tapered, Old Style. Farmers do not know what a bracketed serif is.
Exhibit C: The Growing Pencil
Went into the sharpener. Came out longer. Writes in a green that is not on any crayon box I own.

Exhibit D: The Eraser Message
I erased a BLANK page in the dark and it left glowing script behind. Lovely letterforms, honestly.
Exhibit E: The Beamed Ligature
Saw this over the Kmart parking lot. Two loose letters went up, one ligature came down.
Exhibit F: Moon Kerning
Measured through my telescope with a ruler taped to the lens. Same gap every time. Explain THAT, NASA.
